Injection sites
Where to shoot · L and R are yours, not the diagram's
Tap a numbered zone for how to find it on yourself.
  • Twenty zones, and five injections a day. KP10 rides in the BB20 syringe, so five sites get used. That is four days of rest per zone. Below four days is flagged.
  • NJ1000 and Cu50 are thighs and glutes only. Twenty five units of NAD and a stinging copper peptide both need a deep pad. They cannot be sent anywhere else.
  • Abdomen and flank are one region. It runs from under the ribs down to the belt line, and as far around each side as you can pinch a full fold. Stay two inches clear of the navel and off the waistband itself.
  • Thigh means the outer half only, between the trouser seam and the midline. The strip runs from one hand below the hip bump to one hand above the kneecap. Not the front, never the inside.
  • Glute is the upper outer quarter. Palm on the top of the hip bone at the back, two to three inches down and out. Never the lower or inner quarters.
  • Back of the arm is a real site but a small one. Marked dashed, never suggested first, and awkward to pinch solo.
  • Pinch an inch, 45 to 90 degrees, inject slowly, count to five before withdrawing.
  • Skip anything bruised, red, tender, scarred, or carrying a mole or stretch mark. A slash on a zone means it was used in the last five days.
  • Lateral hip was considered and dropped. It is a standard landmark for intramuscular injection, not a recognised subcutaneous site, so it is not on this map.

Rules that actually matter
  • CP10 needs an empty stomach. Two hours after food, nothing to eat for 30 minutes after. Glucose blunts the GH pulse and wastes the dose.
  • 1G01 is the opposite. Never fasted, never before cardio, always with a carb-containing meal. Keep fast sugar within reach for the first hour.
  • 1G01 runs 20 consecutive days. No 5 on 2 off. In BAC water it degrades on roughly the same timeline it takes to finish the vial, so stretching it means weak final doses.
  • BB20 and KP10 share one syringe and one site. Draw BB20 first, then KP10, inject immediately. Both are plain peptides in BAC water with nothing that reacts. Never pre-draw and let them sit mixed. The cost is a microlitre of carryover between vials from the fixed needle, which is minor but real.
  • Cu50 gets its own syringe and its own site. Copper is redox-active. Fattiest site you have, and it stings.
  • NJ1000 goes in slowly. 30 to 60 seconds, roomy site. Morning only, it disrupts sleep.
  • Reconstitute gently. Water down the inside wall, swirl, never shake. Each compound in its own vial, never combined.
  • Rotate sites every time. Six shots a day saturates the map. Persistent lumps mean the schedule, not your technique.
Watch
  • Two compounds push the IGF axis at once. CP10 raises GH which raises IGF-1, and 1G01 adds exogenous signal on top. Bloodwork before starting and again around week four: IGF-1, fasting glucose, HbA1c.
  • Hypoglycemia is the real 1G01 risk. The hunger is the symptom you notice first. Shakiness, sweating, confusion or odd anxiety means eat immediately.
  • Effects fading late in a vial is the vial. Expect 10 to 20 percent potency loss across 20 days of IGF-1 in BAC water. Do not chase it with a higher dose.
